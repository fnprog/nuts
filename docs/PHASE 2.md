# From Budget App → Personal Finance OS

The difference is fundamental. A budget app asks *"where did my money go?"* A financial OS asks **"what is my complete financial picture, and where am I going?"**

Think of it like this:

> A budget app is a speedometer. A financial OS is the entire dashboard — speed, fuel, engine health, GPS, and a co-pilot telling you the best route.

---

## 🧠 The Core Idea

Every person has a financial life made of **6 layers.** Most apps only touch layer 1. You'd own all 6.

```
Layer 6 │ LEGACY          Wills, inheritance, life insurance
Layer 5 │ PROTECTION      Insurance, emergency fund, risk
Layer 4 │ GROWTH          Investments, crypto, real estate, business
Layer 3 │ DEBT            Loans, credit, repayment strategy
Layer 2 │ CASHFLOW        Income, expenses, budgets (what you're building now)
Layer 1 │ AWARENESS       Net worth, full picture, financial health score
```

Right now you're building Layer 2 really well. The OS plays across all 6.
